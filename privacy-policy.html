Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

At The Auto Aura (theautoaura.in), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, submit booking requests, or use our car wash and detailing services in Kolkata.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out contact and booking forms on our website. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, phone number, email address.
  • Booking Preferences: Type of service selected, preferred date, and any message you choose to provide.
  • Technical Data: Our embedded Google Map may collect technical data and use cookies according to Google's own policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Process, confirm, and schedule your car wash and detailing appointments.
  • Communicate updates, reminders, or questions regarding your booking via phone call, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • Improve our service options and customer support.
  • Maintain a secure booking environment and prevent misuse of the booking form.

3. Data Retention and Security

Booking-form submissions are stored through Supabase, our booking-data service provider. Access is limited to authorized administrators handling bookings. We retain booking details only as long as necessary to fulfill the requested services or legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We share booking details only with service providers needed to operate the booking form, such as Supabase, and only for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Cookies and Third-Party Services

This page embeds Google Maps, which may set cookies or process technical data under Google's policies. We do not currently use Google Analytics or Google Ads conversion tags on this landing page. If this changes, we will update this policy before using those tools.

6. Google Ads Disclosures

If this website is used as a Google Ads destination, we aim to provide clear business information and transparent booking details. In particular:

  • We clearly disclose our physical address, email, and contact details on this website.
  • No false or exaggerated guarantees are made about scratch-resistance or paint longevity.
  • User data collected via form submissions is used to respond to booking requests as described in this policy.
